🔹 What is TF-IDF?

TF-IDF is a statistical method to figure out how important a word is in a document relative to a collection of documents (the corpus).

It improves upon Bag of Words (BoW), which only counts how often a word appears — TF-IDF also considers how unique that word is across all documents.

🧠 Example

Let’s say you have 3 documents:

Document	Text
D1	"I love machine learning"
D2	"I love deep learning"
D3	"Machine learning is fun"

🧩 Intuitive Summary
Method	What it does	Example insight
BoW	Counts word frequency	“love” appears twice
TF-IDF	Weighs words by importance	“machine” matters more than “love”
🧠 Real-Life Analogy

Imagine you’re reading student essays.

The word “the” appears in every essay → not special.

The word “Transformer” appears in only one essay → unique and meaningful.

TF-IDF gives more weight to “Transformer” than to “the”.
